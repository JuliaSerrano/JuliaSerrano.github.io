I've always needed to write things down to process what's really happening.
Even to do a simple math calculation.
I can't think without writing...

Recently, I have been presented to the *Do it in Public* movement, and I think it aligns soo good with my process of thinking and doing things.

Writing is already a part of my learning process so I've already got the first step covered. But I've always lacked organization and structure among the notes I take. I also have a bit of irregularity going on in terms of consistency and a lot of jumping between topics and lacking a clear focus.

I believe that following this trend will help solving some of this issues. At least it might give me a sense of accountability, however fictitious (because let's be honest, how many people are going to read this 😅).

So, here's to a journey of note-taking, posting and learning. Cheers! 🍻
